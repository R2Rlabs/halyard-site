Privacy

Last updated 26 September 2026 · r2rlabs

This page covers halyard.r2rlabs.com as it stands today: lessons, a training deck and a practice trading screen, all free and none of them asking who you are. When real trading opens it will have its own terms and this page will be replaced by something longer. We will say so here when that happens.

What we collect

Nothing about you. There is no sign-up, no form and no account. We do not ask for your name, your email, your country or a wallet address, and there are no advertising pixels or tracking cookies anywhere on this site.

What we count

We do count five things, so we can tell whether anybody is using this at all:

  • the trading screen was loaded
  • a practice position was opened
  • a practice position was closed
  • a practice position was liquidated
  • an order expired without finding the other side

Each one sends a single request carrying the name of the event and two numbers: how much of the simulated market in your browser is long and how much is short, in BTC. Those two numbers are produced by our own code to populate the practice screen, and they describe nobody. Beyond that, nothing: no cookie, no identifier, no session, no account, no page address, no referrer, and nothing about your own position. Our counter adds one to an hourly total and keeps no record of the request, so it can say forty positions were opened today and can never say who opened them — not to us, and not to anyone who asked us.

That is a real limit and we accept it: because there is no identifier, we cannot tell whether forty positions were forty people or one person forty times. Counting people would need something that follows you between visits, and we would rather not have it.

The counter runs on Cloudflare Workers under our own account, so the numbers go to us rather than to an analytics company. Totals are deleted after 90 days. If you block it, nothing on the site changes.

What stays in your browser

The practice screen keeps your play balance, your open position and your trade history in your own browser’s local storage, along with how far you got in the lessons and slides. It never leaves your device and we cannot read it. Clearing your browser data, or pressing “Start again with 10,000”, erases it.

Who else sees anything

  • Cloudflare, which runs the counter described above and therefore sees the request that carries the event name.
  • Our web host, which keeps ordinary server logs, including IP addresses, for a short period for security and abuse handling.
  • The price sources. The live BTC price comes straight from Coinbase or Kraken to your browser, and funding from OKX or Bybit, so those companies see the request and your IP address. We do not send them anything about you.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces and therefore receives your IP address. A blocking extension stops it and the pages still work.

If you email us

Writing to hello@r2rlabs.com or security@r2rlabs.com is the only way we end up with your address. Those mailboxes run on Hostinger, which stores the messages. We keep them until they have served their purpose, we do not add you to any list, and we do not sell, rent or share what you write.

Your choices

  • We send no marketing email at all, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from.
  • Write to hello@r2rlabs.com and ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it. For most visitors the answer is that we hold nothing, and we will say so.
  • Depending on where you live you may have further rights, including with a local data protection authority.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Mail from r2rlabs.com is signed and authenticated (SPF, DKIM and DMARC set to reject), so mail that pretends to be us should be rejected before it reaches you. We will never email asking you to send funds, share a seed phrase or connect a wallet. If you receive such a message, it is not from us: report it to security@r2rlabs.com.
