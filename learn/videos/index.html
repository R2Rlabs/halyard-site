Opening a long

Betting the price goes up · 4.6 MB

Pick the side, put in 500, check the liquidation price, press the button, then sit through the ten seconds it takes to settle on Trac. It fills when a trader takes the other side, and the position appears with its entry marked on the ladder.

Opening a short

Betting the price goes down · 3.9 MB

The same trade in reverse, and watch one number in particular: the moment Short is pressed, the liquidation price jumps from below the market to above it, and the worst price you accept flips the other way too. That is the part people find hardest, and it happens in a single frame.

These are recordings of the practice screen, so the money is play money and the traders on the other side are simulated. Everything else — the prices, the ten-second settlement, the liquidation maths — is the real thing.
