What to try

Push the crowd to 95% long. Most orders stop filling, because there is nobody to pair them with — they wait rather than fill at a bad price.

Now drag the fund to 25,000. More of the gap can be backed, so more orders fill. That is what the fund buys.

Then set the market to falling hard and watch who gets liquidated, and what it costs the fund.

A teaching model, not a forecast: prices are random, every trader risks the same 500 at 5×, and real markets are messier. The rules it follows — pairing by quantity, funding at up to 0.03% an hour, the fund backing an imbalance as if the price could move 20% against it — are Halyard's own.
