How it works

Learn, practice, then trade

Nothing here needs a deposit until you want one.

1. Learn

Six short lessons covering leverage, liquidation, funding and stop-losses. About fifteen minutes, free, no account needed.

2. Practice

10,000 play USDT at real prices, on the same screen as real trading. It runs on your own device, so it costs nothing and risks nothing.

3. Trade

Pass a ten-question experience check, then deposit. Score 9 or 10 and you trade straight away; otherwise the lessons come first.

Halyard is not a spot market. You never own BTC here. Every position is a contract matched against another trader, and when you win, they lose. That is why we make people practice first.
