Why there is no order book

Most exchanges show a ladder of resting buy and sell orders. Halyard has none: your order is matched against a trader taking the opposite side, at the oracle price.

That is why fills wait for the other side, why funding pulls the crowded side back, and why the insurance fund exists at all. In practice you are matched instantly.
